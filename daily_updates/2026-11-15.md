## Added manual capo and the generated chord library
- added a capo on/off button and a fret box (1 to 12) in the camera footer. chords are tracked by sounding pitch; tapping a shape sounds higher by the capo fret, and each suggestion shows the shape to play. the box turns red for invalid input. the setting is not persisted.
- added `frontend/src/theory/voicings.ts`: searches standard tuning for playable fingerings of any chord (root in the bass, chord tones only, at most four fingers, four-fret span, contiguous strings), detects barres, scores by ease, and returns up to four varied options per chord. every root and all 13 qualities have at least one shape.
- replaced the four hardcoded diagrams and tabs with a chord picker (12 roots plus a quality menu), a shape stepper, and a diagram that draws barres and shows a fret number up the neck.
- added `voicings.test.ts`. 40 tests pass in total, the typecheck is clean, and `npm run build` succeeds.
- tuning found by testing: trailing muted strings are penalized, and two fingers on the same lowest fret without a real barre is penalized. the variety filter now treats shapes with different strings muted as different, which keeps the standard barre shapes.
- known gaps: root position only, no inversions or slash chords, difficulty scores are a heuristic and have not been tried by players, and the audio model still only targets C, G, Am, and F.

## Started Phase 2: browser audio chord recognition
- added `frontend/src/audio`: `fft.ts` (hann window and radix-2 fft), `chroma.ts` (spectral peaks with parabolic interpolation folded into 12 pitch classes, plus the lowest strong peak as the bass note), `recognizer.ts` (cosine match against a template for every root and quality, with overtones in the templates, a quality prior, and a bass bonus), `analyze.ts` (one pure function for a block of audio), `stabilizer.ts` (a chord must hold for four frames to count, once per strum), and `useChordListener.ts` (the microphone hook, about ten analyses a second).
- the app now opens the mic with echo cancellation, noise suppression, and auto gain off. the detected chord block shows what is heard live, a real confidence, a level meter, 12 chroma bars, and runner-up chords. a heard chord that holds steady is added to the progression. weak evidence shows Unknown, silence shows a dash.
- removed the fixed 82% demo confidence.
- `synth.ts` builds guitar-like test audio from the chord library fingerings. on it, major, minor, and seventh chords are named correctly in all 12 keys, dim, aug, sus, m7b5, and dim7 are at least 90% once bass detection uses the lowest strong peak (the first version used the loudest low peak and confused chords that share notes), and maj7, m7, m6 and 6 chords are weaker (6 chords are the worst, about 46%).
- 64 tests pass, the typecheck is clean, and the production build succeeds.
- not done: accuracy on a real guitar is unmeasured, the roadmap's trained-model and ONNX parity item was not built (templates were used instead), and the 100 ms loop uses a 0.37 s window, so a chord change takes roughly half a second to settle.
- no git add, commit, or push performed.
